THE BIOGRAPHY

From Madeira to the world's biggest football stages

THE BOY FROM MADEIRA

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born on 5 February 1985 in Funchal, on the Portuguese island of Madeira. He joined Sporting CP's academy as a teenager and moved away from home to pursue football. His senior performances attracted Manchester United, who signed him in 2003.

At United, Real Madrid, Juventus, back at United, and later Al Nassr, Ronaldo repeatedly adapted his game. He began as a creative wide player and developed into a prolific forward known for movement, aerial ability, powerful finishing and an exceptional drive to improve.

THE CAREER JOURNEY

Key chapters in a remarkable football life

1985 — Madeira, Portugal

Ronaldo is born in Funchal. Football becomes his focus from an early age, and his talent takes him from local youth football to Sporting CP's academy.

2002 — Sporting CP first team

He makes his senior breakthrough at Sporting. His speed, dribbling and confidence attract attention from leading European clubs.

2003–2009 — Manchester United

Ronaldo joins United as a teenager. He develops into a world-class attacker, wins three Premier League titles, the 2008 UEFA Champions League and his first Ballon d'Or in 2008.

2009–2018 — Real Madrid

He becomes Real Madrid's all-time leading scorer, wins four Champions League titles with the club and establishes one of the most prolific scoring records in football history.

2018–2021 — Juventus

Ronaldo takes on Serie A, wins two league titles and a Coppa Italia, and continues to score at a high level in a new football environment.

2021–2022 — Manchester United return

He returns to Old Trafford and adds another chapter to his relationship with the club, scoring important goals during his second spell.

2023–present — Al Nassr

Ronaldo begins a new chapter in Saudi Arabia. His arrival draws worldwide attention to the Saudi Pro League and adds a new dimension to his career.

Portugal — International legacy

He captains Portugal to UEFA Euro 2016 and the inaugural UEFA Nations League title in 2019. He becomes the men's international all-time leading goalscorer.

THE CLUB CHAPTERS

Different leagues. Different challenges. One extraordinary career.

SPORTING CP

Sporting's academy shaped Ronaldo's early football education. At the senior level he displayed the acceleration, close control and fearless attacking style that made top clubs take notice. His performance against Manchester United in 2003 helped lead to his transfer to England.

MANCHESTER UNITED

His first United spell transformed him into a global star. He learned to balance flair with end product, developed his physical game and became a decisive scorer. The 2007–08 season brought the Premier League, Champions League and Ballon d'Or.

REAL MADRID

Madrid saw Ronaldo reach extraordinary scoring levels. His off-ball movement, finishing with either foot, heading and ability to deliver in major matches made him central to a historic era, including four Champions League wins with the club.

JUVENTUS

In Italy, Ronaldo adapted to a different tactical environment. He won two Serie A titles and the Coppa Italia, while remaining one of the team's main attacking threats.

PORTUGAL

Ronaldo's international career spans multiple generations. His leadership and goals helped Portugal win Euro 2016 and the 2019 Nations League, the country's first major senior men's international trophies.

AL NASSR

His move to Al Nassr in 2023 brought a new challenge in Saudi Arabia. He continues to compete, score and help raise the international profile of the league.

PORTUGAL LEGEND

LEADING HIS COUNTRY

Ronaldo made his senior Portugal debut in 2003 and went on to become the nation's most-capped player and the men's international all-time leading goalscorer. His international career includes five FIFA World Cup tournaments and multiple UEFA European Championships.

At Euro 2016, Portugal won the final against France to claim its first major senior men's international trophy. In 2019, Portugal won the inaugural UEFA Nations League. Those achievements became defining moments in the country's football history.

His Portugal story is about more than goals: it includes leadership, responsibility, resilience and representing Madeira and the whole country on the biggest stage.
