CRISTIANO RONALDO

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro is a Portuguese professional footballer widely regarded as one of the greatest players in football history.

Born on 5 February 1985 in Funchal, Madeira, Ronaldo developed his football abilities from a young age. His journey began with Sporting CP and took him to Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus and Al Nassr.

He is famous for his goalscoring, athletic ability, heading, speed, powerful shots and determination.
